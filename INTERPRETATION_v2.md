# INTERPRETATION v2 (extension study)

Every number is copied from `results/*.csv` and `results/tables/` (T5.8-T5.11, TF1-TF5, `results/v2/`). Tier labels follow `PREREGISTRATION.md`: H1 is the only primary hypothesis (its outcome was known from v1; see section 0 there), H2 (the lambda contrast across architectures, Regime B) is secondary, and everything else is exploratory.

## 1. Did the leakage finding survive adversarial checking?

**Yes, with a more precise mechanism than the plan assumed and with explicit limits.**

- **Reproduced.** The overlap (211 of Ind_425, 298 of Ind_1049) reproduces under an independent reader with normalised sequences, identically before and after cleaning (`LEAKAGE_FINDING.md`). Chance, parsing, file pasting and a "within-set clustering should have removed it" explanation were each ruled out.
- **Mechanism, verified against the original files.** AIPpred's and AntiInflam's partitions are each internally disjoint (0 exact overlap). The local `Ind_1049` is identical to AIPpred's independent set and `Ind_425` to AntiInflam's validation set; the Benchmark is the AIPpred benchmark plus AntiInflam training sequences (612 of them), and the 37 label conflicts are exactly the sequences where the two lineages disagree. The overlap is a cross-lineage effect, not an error in either dataset.
- **Confirmed by Phase D.** Where the pairing stays inside one lineage (AIPpred benchmark to AIPpred independent; AntiInflam training to AntiInflam validation) Regime A equals Regime B, because nothing overlaps (AAC+RF MCC 0.424 / 0.424 and 0.406 / 0.406). Where the lineages cross, performance falls when the overlap is removed: AAC+RF 0.663 to 0.401 (AIPpred benchmark to AntiInflam validation), 0.511 to 0.322 (AntiInflam training to AIPpred independent), and 0.674 to 0.425 and 0.552 to 0.374 for the combined file.
- **Caveats, stated plainly.** (a) The AIPFusion-Net paper could not be read (HTTP 503; no search trace), so nothing about what it did is verified. (b) AIPStack's independent sets were not obtainable. (c) CD-HIT is a re-implementation, so cluster counts are approximate. (d) The measurement concerns the distributed files; whether any published experiment used exactly these files is not measured. (e) The 211/298 figures are exact-match counts; identity at 0.9 to the cleaned Benchmark gives 61.9% and 31.7%.

## 2. Does the contrastive objective help in ANY of the six architectures, after FDR correction?

**No.** The preregistered rule (BH-adjusted DeLong p < 0.05 and a positive MCC-difference CI lower bound, Regime B) is met by **0 of 10** H2 comparisons (5 architectures x 2 test sets; B1 is the v1 H1 test, which was not supported).

- **H1 (8M MLP, lambda 2, tau 0.1 vs 0):** MCC difference -0.0168 [-0.0869, +0.0556] (Ind_425) and -0.0035 [-0.0484, +0.0455] (Ind_1049); DeLong p 0.77 and 0.80. Not supported.
- **H2 (Regime B, ensemble MCC difference [95% CI], raw DeLong p, BH-adjusted p):** B3 BiLSTM +0.104 [+0.005, +0.207] (p 0.79, BH 0.79) and +0.016 (0.57, 0.77); B4 CNN-BiLSTM +0.127 [+0.009, +0.238] (0.32, 0.65) and -0.004 (0.10, 0.35); B6 attention pooling -0.058 (0.057, 0.28) and +0.062 (0.19, 0.48); B2 CNN -0.028 (0.67, 0.77) and -0.025 (0.014, 0.14); B5 Transformer -0.029 (0.52, 0.77) and -0.037 (0.70, 0.77). Two intervals exclude zero on MCC (B3, B4 on Ind_425) but the AUROC difference is not significant for either (DeLong 0.79 and 0.32); that MCC gain is a threshold/calibration effect at 0.5, not better ranking, so the rule correctly rejects it.
- **All lambda contrasts (56 tests across H1, H2, other regimes, v1 checkpoints):** 14 raw significant, 7 after family BH, 8 after global BH. In Regime B every survivor is negative (150M and 650M on Ind_1049, AUROC -0.023). The only positive survivor is in the leaky Regime A (8M MLP, tau 0.07, Ind_425, AUROC +0.016, global BH p 0.048) and it disappears in Regime B.
- **Cross-benchmark (12 tests):** 1 raw significant (650M, AntiInflam training to AIPpred independent, MCC +0.044 [+0.002, +0.088], p 0.011), none after BH.
- **Nested CV (outer 5 x 5, inner 3-fold lambda selection):** outer MCC 0.2210 at lambda 0 versus 0.2097 with the selected lambda, difference -0.0113 (95% CI -0.0233 to +0.0008, n = 25); the selected lambda varied across folds (0.5 in 7, 2.0 in 6, 0.25 in 5, 1.0 in 4, 0.1 in 3).
- **What does exist.** In-distribution 5 x 5 CV shows a small positive gain at lambda 2 in 5 of 6 architectures (+0.003 to +0.014 MCC; largest B5 Transformer +0.0136, d_z 0.55, and B6 +0.0120). These intervals treat 25 overlapping folds as independent, so they are optimistic, and the gain does not appear on the independent sets.
- **Power.** The minimum detectable effect (80% power, paired) is about 0.14 MCC / 0.046 AUROC on Ind_425 Regime B (n = 214) and about 0.08 MCC / 0.029 AUROC on Ind_1049 (n = 751) (`POWER_ANALYSIS.md`). The cross-validation gains (0.005 to 0.014) are far below that, so the independent sets can neither confirm nor exclude them. The correct statement is "no effect larger than about 0.08-0.14 MCC was detectable", not "no effect".
- **Seed variance exceeds the effects.** The SD of the per-seed MCC difference is 0.015-0.247 across all comparisons of T5.11b, larger than any mean difference.

## 3. Why does AAC beat ESM-2? Which of C1-C3 settled it?

All values are exploratory.

- **C1, pooling: ruled out.** On frozen 650M embeddings the best of six pooling variants is CLS (CV MCC 0.272), against mean pooling 0.237, attention 0.242, mean||max 0.235, max 0.209, first/last 0.195, and AAC+RF 0.429. No variant beats mean pooling after BH in Regime B; first/last-3 and mean||max are significantly worse on Ind_1049.
- **C2, fusion: ruled out as a rescue.** Concatenating ESM-2 with AAC+DPC+CTD does not beat composition alone: RF 5-fold CV MCC 0.411 (hand-crafted) vs 0.384 (8M fusion) and 0.346 (650M fusion); LightGBM 0.377 vs 0.376 and 0.375. In Regime B, fusion minus hand-crafted-only is not significant for RF or LightGBM (for example 650M RF -0.103 [-0.208, -0.009] on Ind_425 and -0.036 [-0.088, +0.016] on Ind_1049, p 0.72 and 0.70). Fusion is much better than ESM-only (e.g. LightGBM +0.14 to +0.25 MCC), so the composition features carry the signal and ESM-2 adds none that these classifiers use. No fusion model significantly beats AAC+RF.
- **C3, fine-tuning: the strongest evidence.** Same head, loop and folds, 8M backbone: CV MCC 0.385 / AUROC 0.739 fine-tuned vs 0.233 / 0.647 frozen. Regime B: +0.220 [+0.072, +0.362] MCC on Ind_425 and +0.150 [+0.075, +0.225] on Ind_1049 (BH-adjusted p 0.022 and < 0.0001). Fine-tuned 8M vs AAC+RF: +0.035 [-0.021, +0.090] on Ind_1049 (p 0.36) but -0.109 [-0.211, -0.007] on Ind_425 (AUROC -0.085, BH p 0.003). Fine-tuned 8M against the frozen 650M model (lambda 0): +0.113 [+0.037, +0.189] MCC on Ind_1049 (AUROC +0.057, BH-adjusted p 0.005) but -0.075 [-0.209, +0.053] on Ind_425 (p 0.59), so the fine-tuned 8M model beats the frozen 650M model on one leakage-free test set and is not distinguishable from it on the other. Adapting the representation closes most of the gap on Ind_1049 and part of it on Ind_425. The Transformer over frozen 8M residue vectors (B5; CV MCC 0.353) is the best frozen-feature architecture and is not significantly different from AAC+RF on Ind_1049 (p 0.077).
- **Dataset construction: partly supported, not proven (F1-F5).** 72.3% of negatives share an 8-mer with another negative versus 13.6% with a positive (positives: 30.3% with another positive, 18.3% with a negative), consistent with overlapping windows from shared parent sequences within the negative class. Sequence length alone gives AUROC 0.696 pooled on the Regime B test sets (0.614 on the training set). Within length bins the models are weak outside 13-16 residues, where all of them are at their best (AAC+RF AUROC 0.729 at 13-16, then 0.583 at 17-20, 0.571 at 21-30 and 0.552 at 7-12; other models range 0.24-0.67 outside 13-16, several below 0.5 at 7-12 and 17-20). Residue enrichment is modest (L, R up; D, G, V, T down; |log2 ratio| <= 0.30; BH-significant) and its rank correlation with the RF importances is weak (Spearman 0.32, p 0.17). Dipeptides SL and LL are the most enriched.
- **Verdict.** Pooling (C1) and fusion (C2) did not explain it; fine-tuning (C3) did most of the work, so frozen mean-pooled embeddings under a small head discard or hide signal that adaptation recovers. Dataset construction plausibly limits the ceiling for every model but was not tested causally.

## 4. Does the null result hold across benchmarks, or only on these two?

It holds on every cell that could be built, with the limits that follow. Phase D crossed three training sets (AIPpred benchmark, AntiInflam training, the combined file) with the two independent sets under all three regimes and 7 models. The lambda contrast (8M and 650M) is not significant in 11 of 12 cells after any correction (ΔMCC from -0.036 to +0.044; 7 positive, 5 negative); the one raw-significant cell does not survive BH. Limits: only two independent test sets exist (the six cells are not independent), AIPStack's sets were not obtainable, and the transfer experiment on other peptide families (D3) was not attempted. A claim about "the field's benchmarks" is not supported beyond these two lineages.

## 5. The single strongest claim the results support (abstract sentence)

"The AIPpred and AntiInflam benchmarks are each internally disjoint but share IEDB-derived peptides across lineages, so a combined training file overlaps their independent sets by 49.6% and 28.4% of sequences; removing that overlap lowers the MCC of composition-based classifiers by 0.18-0.25, and under leakage-free evaluation a supervised contrastive objective did not significantly improve MCC in any of six architectures (0 of 10 preregistered comparisons after FDR correction, minimum detectable effect about 0.08-0.14 MCC), while end-to-end fine-tuning of ESM-2 8M raised MCC by 0.15-0.22 over a frozen backbone."

## 6. The three weakest points an examiner will attack

1. **"No significant difference" is not "no effect".** *Attack:* the test sets are small (n = 214 and 751 after leakage control), so a real 0.01-0.05 MCC gain would be invisible, and the in-distribution CV does show +0.005 to +0.014 at lambda 2. *Honest answer:* agreed; the minimum detectable effect is 0.08-0.14 MCC, the CV gain is below it, its intervals are optimistic, and nested CV removes it (-0.011). State the result as "no detectable effect larger than about 0.08-0.14 MCC", and note that every Regime B survivor of correction is negative.
2. **The best ESM-based result still does not clearly beat a 21-feature Random Forest, and the explanation is only partly tested.** *Attack:* fine-tuning was done for the 8M model only, with one hyper-parameter setting, early stopping on the CV validation fold for both modes, GPU runs, and 3 CV seeds; fusion and pooling were evaluated with one classifier family each, and the "benchmark construction" account (negatives sharing 8-mers, length confound) is correlational. *Honest answer:* fine-tuned 8M vs AAC+RF is -0.109 [-0.211, -0.007] on Ind_425 and +0.035 [-0.021, +0.090] on Ind_1049, so the gap is closed on one set only; claim "adaptation matters" rather than "ESM-2 beats composition", and flag larger-model fine-tuning as future work (it needs a GPU).
3. **Method deviations and a partly retrospective preregistration.** *Attack:* residue-level models use a 64-d learned bottleneck and 64 hidden units (plan: 128), B2/B5 were trained on a different device from B3/B4/B6, the temperature was fixed at 0.07 for Phase B, lambda sat on the edge of the grid, H1 was already known when the preregistration was committed, and the AIPFusion-Net premise could not be verified. *Honest answer:* each deviation is logged before its results were seen (`DECISIONS_v2.md`), every architecture's lambda grid is on a single device, the preregistration says openly that H1 was known, and the leakage finding is stated only as a measurement on the distributed and original files, with the paper-specific statements marked unverified.

Also open: C4 (other protein language models) and D3 (AMP/ACP transfer) were optional and not attempted (`BLOCKERS_v2.md`); Table 5.7 (published predictors) still has no verified published numbers.
